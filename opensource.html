EffectFactory

An open-source generator of video effect assets: light particles, focus lines, fog, rain and more.

  • Python
  • Tkinter
  • NumPy
  • Pillow
  • FFmpeg
  • MIT

Preview, MP4 export and loop generation. Random seeds and settings JSON keep every render reproducible, and new effects plug in as plain Python files.

Earth History Globe 地球型歴史地図

A public prototype for learning and visualization: browse borders by era, plus Earth and life history, on a 3D globe.

  • Three.js
  • GeoJSON
  • Electron
  • Python
  • JavaScript
  • MIT code
  • GPL-3.0 data

Era slider, autoplay, place details, and an amount of detail that adjusts to viewing distance. It also notes the sources, licenses and uncertainty of the border data.
